# Overview
A quick and dirty tool for solving the Pokemon related question: what 4 move types are best for maximizing effectiveness against as many Pokemon types as possible? It may be worth coming back to this at some later point to expand the scope a little bit and make it more generally useful (applying restrictions, choosing less than 4 moves, maybe even other similar games/situations), but as it is, it's just this.

If you are curious about the answer by the way, it's one of:
- Fighting Flying Ground Ice
- Fighting Ground Ghost Ice (no bad match-ups)
- Fighting Ground Ice Dark
